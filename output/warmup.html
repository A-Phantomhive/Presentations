Teacher notes and sources

This is an adapted, fixed-paced classroom demonstration. It records no individual responses, reaction times or personal data and calculates no IAT score. Feeling slower does not diagnose prejudice. Vocabulary, practice and rule switching can affect the experience.

The flower/insect words are drawn from the classic study; evaluation words are simplified for this class. Pairings are called A and B without promising which will feel easier. Order can be changed for another class, but this does not counterbalance order within a single group.

Greenwald, McGhee & Schwartz (1998) · Best research practices for the IAT
